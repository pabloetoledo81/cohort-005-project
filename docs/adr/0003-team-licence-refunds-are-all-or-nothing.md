# Team Licence refunds are all-or-nothing, and Seats freeze while one is Open

A Team Licence is refunded in full or not at all. Cadence has no per-Seat price, and a Claimed Seat belongs to another student, so there is no fair partial amount to give back for the Seats nobody used. A Team Licence with any Claimed Seat therefore can't be refunded, and its Team Admin can't raise a Refund Request for it. To stop a Seat being claimed between the request and the decision, a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed while it has an Open Refund Request. If the request is declined they work again. If it's approved they can never be claimed.

An Individual Purchase is different: the Platform Admin chooses the amount, so it can have a Partial Refund. The all-or-nothing rule is about Team Licences, not about Refunds in general.

## Considered Options

- **Let Seats be claimed while Open, and only allow Decline afterwards**: rejected, because the Team Admin's request would be quietly defeated by a colleague.
- **Refund part of the money for the Unclaimed Seats**: rejected, because it needs a per-Seat price, and the price of a Team Licence isn't a sum of Seat prices.
- **Let the Platform Admin choose a Team Licence's amount, as for an Individual Purchase**: rejected for the same reason: without a per-Seat price, any figure for the unused Seats would be arbitrary.
